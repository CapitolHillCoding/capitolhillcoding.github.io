Privacy policy

Capitol Hill Coding collects no data at all. Here are the details.

Effective November 12, 2026

Capitol Hill Coding is a coding game for children. It is built to work entirely on your device. We don't collect, store or share any personal information, from children or anyone else, because none of it ever reaches us.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts or sign-ins, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party tracking or data-collecting SDKs. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Her code, progress and settings

Everything your child makes (the programs for each level, and the movies and games in the Studio), their progress, stars, and the voice and music switches are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They never leave it, and nobody else can read them.

The code she writes

Programs written in the app are run by the app itself, on the device. They can't reach the internet, files, the camera or anything else outside the game. The app doesn't download or run code from anywhere.

The keyboard

The code editor can use your device's keyboard. What is typed stays in the app. The keyboard itself belongs to your device; if you have installed a third-party keyboard, its own privacy policy applies to it.

Voices

The characters' lines are read aloud by your device's built-in speech voice. Capitol Hill Coding hands the words to that voice on the device and doesn't record or send anything. The app never listens: it doesn't use the microphone.

Purchases

The app has no ads and nothing for sale inside it. If that ever changes, the App Store or Google Play would handle any payment, and this page would be updated first.

Permissions

None. Capitol Hill Coding doesn't use the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or internet.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to review, share or delete. This is true for children under 13 (COPPA), and in every country.

Deleting your data

Deleting Capitol Hill Coding from your device removes all of its data.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
